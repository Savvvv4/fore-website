About FORE

We started FORE because we love golf.

Golf has given us a lot. The people we've met. The places we've played. The coaches who've helped us. The hours spent practising, competing, learning and simply enjoying being out there.

Who we're building for

FORE is...

01 / THE GOLFER

For the golfer trying to get better.

You might be picking up a club for the first time. You might be playing every weekend. Wherever you are, there is always a next step. We want to make it easier to find yours.

02 / THE COACH

For the coach who wants to help people improve.

Coaches spend too much time finding students and managing everything around the lesson. We want to make it easier for the right golfers to find them.

03 / THE FACILITY

For the people who create places to play.

Ranges, academies, courses and facilities are where golfers meet, practise, learn and become part of the game. We want to help these places become easier to discover, easier to run and more connected.

04 / THE NEXT GENERATION

For the next generation of golfers.

Every golfer starts somewhere. A first swing. A first lesson. A first tournament. Behind each one is usually a parent, a coach or a facility. We want to help more of those journeys happen.

The founders

Two perspectives. One idea.

Savdeep Kadian
SK

Savdeep Kadian

Founder & CTO

I've been playing golf for more than ten years.

I started with a club a coach lent me and spent my early years in junior training camps. I gradually worked my way through the national junior and amateur circuits, and I still aspire to pursue golf professionally.

I've played at courses and facilities across the country. I've met juniors, amateurs, professionals, coaches, parents and people picking up a club for the first time.

And everywhere I've gone, I've seen the same thing: there is a lot of golf out there, but very little connecting it.

A lot of the time, you find things because someone happens to tell you about them.

A coach recommendation. A better facility. A junior program. A tournament. A piece of equipment. The right place to practise.

You get lucky.

I've spent more than a decade trying to find my way through golf.

Now I want to make that journey easier for the next golfer.

Itish Arora
IA

Itish Arora

Founder & CEO

My journey into golf started as a parent trying to help my son navigate the game.

Like many parents, I found myself constantly searching for answers. Which coach was right? Which facilities offered the best opportunities? What tournaments should he play? How do you measure progress? What should come next?

The more involved I became, the more I realized how difficult it was to find reliable information. So much of the journey depended on who you knew, who you could ask and whether someone happened to point you in the right direction.

Golf already has talented coaches, great facilities and passionate people. What was missing was a way to connect them.

My professional journey has been across sales, partnerships and operations, working with people and businesses to build, grow and run organisations. I saw an opportunity to bring that experience to a game that I had become deeply invested in personally.

We're building a platform that makes golf easier to discover and navigate, whether you're a beginner, a competitive player, a parent supporting a junior golfer, a coach or a facility.

Where we're starting

We're starting here.
India → the world.

FORE is being built in India. That's not an accident.

This is the golf community we know. These are the golfers, coaches, parents, academies, ranges and facilities we've spent time with.

We've seen the enthusiasm for the game. We've seen the talent. We've seen people building great things with limited tools. And we've seen how much opportunity there is to make the experience around the golfer better.

So we're starting here. We'll learn here. We'll build with this community. And if we do it well, we believe what we learn can travel much further.
